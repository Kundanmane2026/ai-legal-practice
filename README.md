# AI in Legal Practice

**A free 28-day course for advocates and law students in India on using AI assistants safely in legal work. Available in English, Hindi and Marathi.**

🔗 **Start the course:** https://kundanmane2026.github.io/ai-legal-practice/

## About the course

Fifteen minutes a day of real legal work done with an AI assistant: drafting notices, reading contracts, summarising judgments, and checking every line before it leaves your desk.

Every lesson includes:

- a short explanation grounded in Indian law and practice, with statutes and leading cases
- a ready-to-copy prompt you can adapt
- a practice task on anonymised work
- a **"Verify before you rely"** check
- a **daily playbook entry**, so that by Day 28 each learner has built their own AI practice playbook

No sign-up, no fees, no subscription. Learners' notes are saved only in their own browser; nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

## The FIJO method

The course's core prompting framework for legal work:

| | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | Facts | Tenant has not paid rent for 7 months |
| **I** | Issue | Can the landlord seek eviction and arrears together? |
| **J** | Jurisdiction | Maharashtra; Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 |
| **O** | Output | One-page note with headings and open questions |
